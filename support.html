Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel a subscription?

Tap on any subscription card in SubShield and tap "Cancel Shortcut" or use the 1-Tap Cancel Hub. For Apple subscriptions, tap "Apple Subscriptions" to instantly open your iPhone's Apple ID subscription settings.

How does SubShield protect my financial privacy?

SubShield operates 100% offline and local-first on your device. We never ask for your bank login credentials, never connect to third-party data aggregators, and never sell your financial records. Read our full Privacy Policy.

How do I restore my Elite purchase?

If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new iPhone, open the Upgrade / Elite screen and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple will verify your Apple ID receipt and restore your access immediately.

How do I request a refund for an accidental renewal?

Open the Refund Hub inside the app. For App Store purchases, tap "Apple App Store Refund" to submit a claim directly through Apple's official 48-hour dispute portal (reportaproblem.apple.com).
